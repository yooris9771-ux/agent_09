# AI Development Research ② Social and Economic Impact

> Research date: 2026-10-04 · Topic: AI development

## 1. Employment and the Labor Market

- **Employment impact turned modestly negative**: a reversal from the positive outlook of 2025, yet the headcount reductions firms reported averaged close to zero for both 2025 and 2026 (Richmond Fed CFO Survey).
- **Slower hiring is the main channel**: AI affects jobs mainly by reducing new hires rather than through mass layoffs. Of the job cuts US employers announced from January to August, 116,175 (about 22% of the total) cited AI.
- **Worker perceptions (Gallup, February 2026, 23,717 US workers)**
  - 18% said it is likely their job will be eliminated by AI/automation within five years (23% in AI-adopting organizations).
  - 65% of workers in AI-adopting organizations said AI has helped their productivity and efficiency.
- **Why firms adopt AI**: process efficiency (64%) and employee productivity (59%) > headcount reduction (24%).
- Cognizant report: AI can handle $4.5 trillion worth of US work tasks and could affect up to 93% of jobs.
- Experts and the public differ by 50 percentage points on whether AI will help people do their jobs (AI Index 2026).

## 2. Energy and Environment

- Data center electricity demand grew 17% in 2025—more than five times the 3% growth in global electricity demand (IEA).
- Big Tech's AI infrastructure spending exceeded $400 billion in 2025 and could rise roughly another 75% in 2026.
- IEA outlook: data center electricity demand will double by 2030, and AI-related demand will triple.
- An earlier IEA estimate (published 2024) put 2026 electricity use by data centers, AI, and crypto at 620–1,050 TWh; the upper end is comparable to Japan's total consumption.
- Energy efficiency per task improves, but total consumption keeps rising.

## 3. Science and Medicine

- **AI drug discovery**: as of July 2026, no drug discovered or designed by AI has received full FDA approval.
  - Most advanced case: Insilico Medicine's **rentosertib**, which entered a Phase III trial for idiopathic pulmonary fibrosis in July 2026.
  - 2026 ASCO analysis: 117 AI-enabled therapeutic assets from 63 companies entered human trials; 60 completed Phase 1 and 8 completed Phase 2.
- **Medical devices**: over 400 FDA-cleared AI medical devices as of early 2026 (figures vary by source). In August 2026, the FDA released a discussion paper on a risk-proportionate regulatory approach for generative-AI medical devices.
- AI drug discovery market: $6.93B in 2025 → projected $17.81B by 2035 (9.9% CAGR).
- Peer-reviewed papers on agentic platforms for autonomous drug discovery have also appeared.

## 4. Safety and Trust

- Documented AI incidents rose from 233 to 362 (AI Index 2026).
- Only about half of US middle and high schools have AI policies → safety, policy, and education are lagging behind the pace of technology.
